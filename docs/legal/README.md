# Legal and policy drafts

**These are working drafts written by an engineering assistant. They are not legal advice and have not been reviewed by a lawyer.**
Every `[PLACEHOLDER]` must be filled and the whole set reviewed by counsel before any public release. Minors, location data, virtual
currency and user-generated content are the high-risk areas (see `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`).

- `PRIVACY.md`: what the app actually collects today (matches the schema) and what it does not.
- `TERMS.md`: community rules and account terms.
- `MODERATION.md`: how reports, safety escalations and integrity flags are handled (matches the tools in `scripts/moderation.ts`).
- `STORE_CHECKLIST.md`: Google Play and Apple items that block release, with what is already done.
